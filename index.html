For group admins

The bot never joins your WhatsApp group and never reads it. Members choose what to send.

  • Share the bot's number in your group description so members know where to forward.
  • Nothing about your group changes: no bot to add, no settings to switch.

Coming later: weekly summaries to share with the group and an admin page for managing listings.

Your number stays off the board

The board shows your WhatsApp name, your neighborhood, and when you listed an item. It never shows your phone number.

To create listings, the messages and photos you send to the bot are processed by Meta, which runs WhatsApp, and by an AI service. The person running this board can also see them. We don't sell your information or use it for anything else.

This is an early prototype, so features and safeguards are still being built. Don't send anything you wouldn't want a neighbor to see.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. The board is free for neighbors, and it's only for items given away for free.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Everything happens in WhatsApp. The board opens in your browser from a sign-in link the bot sends you.

How do I see the board?

Open the board link your group shares, or ask the bot for it.

What if I forget to mark something as gone?

Tell the bot whenever you remember. Automatic reminders for older listings are on the way.

Who can see what I send?

Your messages and photos pass through Meta and an AI service to create listings, and the person running the board can see them. The board itself never shows your phone number.
